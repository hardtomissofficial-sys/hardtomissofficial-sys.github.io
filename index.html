About

Hard To Miss is an alternative rock project from Liverpool, creating guitar-driven songs influenced by bands such as Buzzcocks, Queens of the Stone Age, The Kinks and Nirvana.

The music combines driving guitars, melodic hooks and honest songwriting, with an emphasis on keeping things raw, direct and real.

Hard To Miss is independently written, recorded and released, with new music continuing to follow throughout 2026.
