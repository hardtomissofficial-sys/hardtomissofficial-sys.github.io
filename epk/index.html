ABOUT

Hard To Miss is a Liverpool alternative rock project, returning to music after a long history of playing, recording and touring with signed bands.

The story began in the late 1990s with The Moggs, who were signed, recorded an album, toured the UK and received radio play before their label folded. The band later evolved into The Moonies, releasing singles and an EP produced by Gordon Raphael, as well as an album in Japan. They toured the UK several times, including two tours supporting Buzzcocks.

After stepping away from music as work and family life took priority, the project returned in a very different form. Around 2012, Hard To Miss developed a one-man live setup combining a modified guitar with foot-triggered drums, leading to performances including the Liverpool Music Awards and local shows.

Now back in the studio, Hard To Miss is about getting the songs out there simply because making music brings joy. The project draws on alternative, indie and guitar-driven rock, with an emphasis on melodic hooks, raw guitars and honest songwriting.
